# Governance

OARMI is an open research and interoperability project maintained in the public interest. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by IETF, OpenTelemetry, FinOps Foundation, or any vendor referenced in its materials.

## Principles

- Neutrality across providers and frameworks.
- Source transparency and version pinning.
- Clear separation of observations, proposals, estimates, and normative claims.
- Conservative handling of financial/accounting semantics.
- No claims of conformance or certification without a defined process and evidence.

## Change process

Changes are proposed through issues or pull requests. Maintainers may request source evidence, additional fixtures, or narrower claims. Breaking fixture format changes must be documented and versioned.

## Labels of evidence

- **Source review:** documentation reviewed; no implementation tested.
- **Fixture validation:** repository-local checks passed.
- **Implementation observation:** behavior observed in a named version/environment.
- **Independent review:** review performed by parties not authoring the change.

These labels are not interchangeable. This repository currently contains draft material and source-review-level notes only.
